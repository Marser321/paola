# Meta Ads — prompts de animación

Usar solo una pieza animada a la vez dentro de la tira.

## `sample-meta-ads-1.png` · prioridad C

```text
4-second seamless loop. A single warm-gold signal travels slowly from the broad upper campaign stage through the connected rails toward the narrow conversion stage. Two small testing nodes at the side illuminate one after another, then return to their original state. All physical cards, fabric thumbnails and camera remain perfectly still. No new nodes or labels.
```

## `sample-meta-ads-2.png` · prioridad A

```text
4-second seamless loop. The finger performs one slow upward swipe; the camel coat card moves by no more than one third of the phone screen and the charcoal garment enters from below. Phone and camera remain locked. The movement eases back invisibly to the opening frame. No social interface or text appears.
```

## `sample-meta-ads-3.png` · prioridad A

```text
4-second seamless loop. The winning stepped column gains one restrained gold segment while the hand slides the physical budget token a few centimetres toward it. The efficiency line remains stable. At the loop point both return softly to their exact initial positions without a jump.
```

Append the common negative prompt to each.
