# UGC & Creatividades — prompts de animación

## `sample-ugc-1.png` · prioridad B

```text
4-second seamless loop. Animate only the three storyboard frames: one serum drop descends, one hand turns the bottle cap a few degrees, and one fingertip touches the cheek. Actions occur sequentially. The blank script sheet, clips, phone rig, lamp and camera stay frozen. Preserve bottle identity across all cards.
```

## `sample-ugc-2.png` · prioridad A

```text
4-second seamless loop. Three of the nine skincare thumbnails animate one at a time: a serum drop falls, a hand turns the bottle, and a water bead moves down the glass. The remaining six frames and the entire grid remain still. Never crossfade or update the whole contact sheet.
```

## `sample-ugc-3.png` · mantener estática

El orden por retención debe poder compararse. Versión opcional:

```text
4-second seamless loop. A very narrow warm edge light travels from the last fashion card toward the first and settles on the winning coat close-up. Cards, people and fabric remain completely still. No card changes size or position.
```

Append the common negative prompt to each generated clip.
