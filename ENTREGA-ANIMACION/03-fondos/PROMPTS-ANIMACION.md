# Fondos de sección — prompts de animación

Estos clips se reproducen detrás del contenido al 16–42 % de opacidad. El movimiento debe
ser más lento que en las tarjetas: 6 segundos, 24 fps, bucle perfecto.

## `bg-metricas-source.png` · prioridad A

```text
6-second seamless cinemagraph. Preserve the supplied image exactly. On the two monitors only, unlabelled chart bars rise by a few pixels and one thin line advances slowly, then returns without a visible reset. Candle or practical lights may flicker by less than five percent. The woman, chair, desk, room and camera remain perfectly still. No readable figures or new interface content.
```

## `bg-servicios-source.png` · prioridad C

6-second seamless cinemagraph. Preserve the supplied image exactly. Only the stylus hand makes one very short continuous writing stroke on the tablet and returns to the starting point; two nearby plant leaves drift a few millimetres. Head, face, torso, desk and camera remain perfectly still. No content appears on the tablet.
```

## `bg-proceso-source.png` · prioridad A — principal momento ambiental

6-second seamless cinematic loop. Preserve the supplied corridor, woman and warm grade. Add an almost imperceptible forward camera drift below one percent, with no perspective jump. Vertical corridor lights stay steady. Return gait and camera to the exact first frame without reversing visibly.
```
